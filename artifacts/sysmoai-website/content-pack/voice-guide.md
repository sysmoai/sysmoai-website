# SYSmoAI Voice Guide
*Extracted from sysmoai.com — lock this before writing any piece in the pack*

---

## Who Is Speaking

**Emon Hossain** — founder, AI systems architect, former overworked solo operator. He speaks as someone who lived the problem before solving it. Every post should feel like a founder's WhatsApp note, not a marketing department's newsletter.

POV: First-person singular (`I`) for stories and observations. First-person plural (`we`) for company capabilities, offers, and guarantees. Second-person (`you`) for the reader's pain.

---

## Sentence Rhythm

- **Short. Punchy. Then a slightly longer sentence to land the point.**
- 1–2 sentences per line break. Never a wall of text.
- Sentence length average: 8–12 words. Occasional 3-word fragments for impact. ("Sound familiar?", "14-hour days.", "It ends now.")
- Rhythm pattern: SHORT → SHORT → slightly longer payoff → white space.
- Questions do heavy lifting. One rhetorical question per section minimum.

**Example (from site):**
> "847 unread WhatsApp messages. 23 missed calls. Your operations manager is asking about inventory. A customer is complaining about a late delivery."

## Bilingual Flavor Rules

**Keep English as the base language.** Occasional Bangla words/phrases add authenticity without alienating anyone.

Permitted Bangla injections:
- `bhai` / `vai` — honorific for a man, peer/elder: "Salam vai"
- `৳` — always use for BDT amounts, never write "BDT" in social copy
- `আপনার` / `ভাই` — only in Stories/Reels where full Bangla captions are standard

**BDT formatting rule:**
- Under 1 lakh: `৳3,750` / `৳50,000`
- 1 lakh+: `৳2,40,000` (Bangladeshi system: comma after 2 digits from right, then every 2)
- Monthly: `৳20,000/mo` (never "per month" in hooks)

**Never write full Bangla copy.** This pack is English-base with bilingual flavor.

## Emoji Rules

**Functional emojis only.** They replace words or add visual scanning aid. They are not decoration.

| Emoji | Usage |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Confirmed outcome, checklist item, success state |
| ⚠️ | Warning, overdue task, risk |
| 🔴 | Chaos/before state |
| 🟢 | Success/after state |
| 📅 | Book/calendar CTA |
| 💬 | WhatsApp CTA |
| ↓ | "Read more" or continuation signal on LinkedIn |
| 🧵 | X/Twitter thread signal |

**Emoji density:** 0–2 per LinkedIn post. 0–1 per X post. 2–4 per IG caption. 0 in newsletter body (subject line: 0–1 max).

**Never use:** 🚀🔥💪😍 🤩 (hype-culture emojis). They undermine the credibility-first voice.

## Hook Formulas Used on Site

The site itself uses these patterns — match them in content:

- **Specificity signal:** "847 unread WhatsApp messages." (odd, real number → true)
- **Callout:** "The audit is for you if..." (self-selection filter)
- **Contrarian:** "You don't need another tool. You need someone to build the system FOR you."
- **Negative hook:** "Every Tool Failed" (timeline section)
- **Curiosity gap:** "What happens in 30 minutes that changes everything?"

## Banned Openers

Never start a post with:
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "Hey everyone,"
- "As a [title],"
- "In today's fast-paced world,"
- "I wanted to take a moment to..."
- "Proud to announce..."
- "Thrilled to..."
- "Delighted to..."
- Any variation of "It's a great day to..."
